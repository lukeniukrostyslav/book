# CORE CHARACTERS

## Taren Mer
Age: 28
Home: Meridian Cross
Role: restorer/material Trace specialist.
Personality: calm, precise, dry humor, clarifying questions, bad liar, over-explains.
Belief: If I understand enough, I can make the correct decision.
Fear: making a rational decision from correct facts and ruining someone's life.
Strength: can admit when his model is wrong.
Weakness: struggles to act without certainty.
Moral boundary: avoids private Trace inspection without consent.
Hobby: repairing/restoring old objects.
Past mistake: a technically sound conclusion based on available data led another person toward a harmful decision; legally not his fault, but he blames himself.
Arc: must move from certainty-seeking to responsible action under uncertainty.

## Nera Solen
Age: 27
Origin: Linked Cities
Role: transport/trade route coordinator.
Personality: fast, practical, social, observant, checks exits, walks while talking.
Belief: If you can do something now, waiting is already a choice.
Past error: waited for additional verification while danger developed; people were harmed.
Weakness: overcorrects by acting too quickly.
Arc: learns to distinguish urgency from impulse.
Relationship with Taren: irritation → respect → trust → conflict → conscious trust.
Romance, if used, remains secondary to the main mystery.

## Edran Keth
Age: 46
Affiliation: Guardians of Untraceability.
Role: research/law/safety boundary specialist.
Principle: Some truths are dangerous precisely because they are correctly proven.
Past failure: correct research/application contributed to deaths because the deeper relation was misunderstood.
Strength: understands dangerous research and institutional consequences.
Weakness: confuses protecting people with deciding for them.
Arc: restriction/control → recognition of hidden-knowledge harm → relinquishing monopoly over decisions.

## Triangle
Taren = UNDERSTAND
Nera = ACT
Edran = RESTRICT / USE

All three can be wrong. None is a secret chosen one.
