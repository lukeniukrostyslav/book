# SECONDARY CHARACTERS

## Orven Mer
58. Taren's father. Master restorer. Quiet, practical. Family maxim: "Don't throw away a thing until you understand why it broke."

## Sela Mer
55. Taren's mother. Customer-facing work, emotionally perceptive, notices when Taren hides behind technical work.

## Lio Mer
22. Taren's younger sister. Social, community-oriented, represents ordinary life outside the investigation.

## Mira Solen
About 50. Senior figure in Nera's transport/trade network. Practical. Believes Nera does not have to save everyone.

## Joren Vale
31. League specialist. Evidence/system believer. Conducts unauthorized archive research to challenge gaps in League records. Red herring, not central villain.

## Halven Orr
39. Incomplete School specialist. Honest about uncertainty. Distinguishes known, probable and unknown.

## Cael Keth
22–24. Edran's adult child. Relationship with Edran is polite but distant because Cael feels Edran repeatedly decided what others were allowed to know.

## Character consistency rule
Track stable facts separately from changing state: knowledge, trust, location, promises, current goals, injuries, relationships and possessions.
