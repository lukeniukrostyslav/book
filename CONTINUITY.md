# CONTINUITY CONTROL

## Canon hierarchy
1. Explicit CANON files and locked decisions.
2. PROJECT_STATE.
3. Book architecture and character/world bibles.
4. Working hypotheses.
5. Chat discussion that has not been promoted to repository.

## State tracking
For every important scene track location, date/time, character knowledge, character trust, goals, promises, injuries, possessions, evidence status and interpretation status.

## Knowledge separation
FACT = directly observed or verified.
INFERENCE = interpretation supported by facts.
HYPOTHESIS = possible explanation.
UNKNOWN = unresolved.

## No retroactive omniscience
Characters cannot know information merely because the author knows it.

## Travel
No instant travel. Terrain, weather, roads, river crossings and institutional restrictions matter.

## Trace continuity
Trace is evidence of a relation, not automatic proof of motive, guilt, ownership or meaning.

## Character limits
Taren has no unexplained supernatural ability.
Nera is not a Trace specialist.
Edran is not omniscient.

## Canon promotion
A working idea becomes canon only after originality, causality, civilization/world integration and Book 1 fair-play checks, followed by an explicit decision log entry.
