# MASTER PLAN — ORIGINAL FANTASY IP

## Objective
Build an original fantasy IP designed for long-term development across 10–20 years, beginning with a high-quality Book 1 and expandable five-book architecture.

## Production sequence
1. Research and market/originality study
2. Story engine
3. World mechanism
4. Magic/Trace rules
5. Cosmology
6. Civilizations and cultures
7. Geography/ecology
8. Character system
9. Series architecture
10. Book 1 architecture
11. Chapter blueprint
12. Scene blueprint
13. Continuity/clue audit
14. Drafting specification
15. Repository-controlled manuscript drafting
16. Book 1 full draft
17. Structural/editing/continuity QA
18. Final Book 1
19. Illustrations and visual package
20. Expansion to Book 2+

## Core creative rule
Build the franchise around world + characters + mystery + emotional engine, not short-lived trope cycles.

## Current Book 1 premise
A skilled restorer who can verify relationships between objects discovers a real Trace between two things that never interacted. Investigation proves the phenomenon is genuine, historical, and outside known relation classes.

## Long arc
Book 1: impossible Trace.
Book 2: different civilizations see different aspects of reality.
Book 3: why attempts to unify models failed.
Book 4: danger of manipulating deeper structure.
Book 5: responsibility under an incomplete model of reality.
