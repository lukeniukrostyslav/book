## Current originality assessment
Overall architecture: 96% confidence as an original combination, not a claim of absolute uniqueness.

## Known conceptual risk
The philosophical idea that relational/structural organization may be more fundamental than objects exists independently in structural realism / relational ontology. The fiction must not simply reproduce a philosophical theory.

## Differentiating mechanism
The distinctive fiction engine is:
Trace as a practical civilization-wide technology + competing institutional interpretations + fair-play investigation + structural transition that cannot be represented as a conventional event.

## Originality rules
1. Do not turn Deep Structure into a conscious god.
2. Do not say simply that objects do not exist.
3. Do not make Taren uniquely capable of perceiving truth.
4. Do not make the unknown relation solve every mystery.
5. Do not make the ending “everything is connected.”
6. Keep structural transition mechanically constrained.
7. Continue checking new concepts against existing works before canonization.
