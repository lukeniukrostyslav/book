# BOOK 1 — FINAL ORIGINALITY STRESS TEST

Status: PRE-LOCK

## Test A — Familiar mystery
Risk: "lost/erased event."
Result: mitigated. The current design does not rely on forgotten history; it points toward a possible structural transition.

## Test B — Chosen one
Risk: protagonist uniquely selected by reality.
Result: rejected. Taren is a professional investigator with no metaphysical privilege.

## Test C — Hidden god
Risk: Deep Structure is secretly a conscious deity.
Result: rejected. No conscious intelligence is required or established.

## Test D — Everything is connected
Risk: relational premise collapses into a generic slogan.
Result: rejected as final answer. Relations have classes, limits, evidence and institutional consequences.

## Test E — Magic solves plot
Risk: Trace becomes universal problem-solving.
Result: rejected. Trace cannot create matter, rewrite history or guarantee meaning.

## Test F — Philosophical copy
Risk: structural realism becomes the entire fictional identity.
Result: mitigated through practical Trace technology, legal systems, cultures, fair-play investigation and character ethics. Philosophical similarity remains a monitoring risk.

## Test G — Fantasy trope camouflage
Risk: standard fantasy world with new terminology.
Result: currently low-to-moderate. Continued checks required during drafting.

## Final assessment
Book 1 has a differentiated story mechanism, but originality is not considered permanently "proven." Every major new plot device must pass the same test.
