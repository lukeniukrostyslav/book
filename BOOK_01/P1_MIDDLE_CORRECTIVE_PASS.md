# BOOK 1 — P1 CORRECTIVE PASS
## Chapters 19–25 — Middle pacing and repetition

Status: ACTIVE / editorial correction map locked
Date: 2026-09-19

## Objective

Reduce repetitive investigation beats without removing evidence, clue planting, or fair-play logic.

The current Chapters 19–25 are structurally correct. The issue is rhythmic repetition: several chapters use the same sequence of field check → Trace measurement → hypothesis → Nera question → Taren qualification → written fact/hypothesis. The correction therefore changes scene function and texture rather than cutting required evidence.

This approach is consistent with scene-level revision guidance: each scene should have a concrete purpose, move the story forward, and vary pacing/texture; repeated dialogue and repetitive beats should be removed where they do not add new information. citeturn1search0turn1search3

## Chapter 19 — Territory of Layers
Current function: introduce dense historical layering and propose density as a possible factor.

Correction:
- Keep the physical discovery of layered construction.
- Reduce explanatory back-and-forth after the first Trace comparison.
- Make Nera notice the practical consequence first: the current route is physically dependent on several historical layers.
- Taren's density hypothesis should appear only after the three-site comparison, not as repeated confirmation.
- End on a concrete investigative objective: find a low-density control case.
- Preserve the important distinction: correlation, not cause.

New scene texture:
Nera tests the route with a small load/worker movement while Taren measures, making the setting functional rather than purely observational.

## Chapter 20 — Control
Current function: low-density control and first counterexample.

Correction:
- Enter later: start with the unexpected Trace already found.
- Remove repeated instrument-by-instrument explanation unless a method changes the conclusion.
- Make the surprise physical: the supposedly simple wall has an old route mark that forces a new comparison.
- Taren should downgrade density in the field, not only in notebook exposition.
- End with a concrete unanswered question rather than another abstract summary.

## Chapter 21 — Common Feature
Current function: compare four cases and identify “change of configuration” as a candidate common feature.

Correction:
- Turn the chapter into an active reconstruction session.
- Taren and Nera physically rearrange four case cards/maps on the workshop floor.
- Nera should challenge the classification from practical route knowledge.
- Taren notices the commonality only after removing object names and materials.
- Keep one concise fact/hypothesis distinction; eliminate multiple repetitions of “not proof”.
- The final line should point directly to the next required test.

## Chapter 22 — Warning
Current function: Edran introduces a historical analogue and a negative case.

Correction:
- Make this primarily an Edran character scene, not another research lecture.
- His correction of the word “preserves” remains essential.
- Add a brief consequence of his earlier secrecy: Taren recognizes that Edran withheld evidence because he feared unsafe experimentation, but does not yet forgive him.
- The old case should be presented through Edran's controlled disclosure.
- End with Edran granting access to the negative case.

This gives the chapter emotional and character function in addition to epistemic function.

## Chapter 23 — Negative Case
Current function: hypothesis is constrained by a genuine negative case.

Correction:
- Preserve the negative result and the three follow-up checks.
- Compress repeated “change happened / no Trace” confirmations.
- Give the reader one concrete engineering detail showing that the old system was actually severed.
- Taren should visibly cross out the earlier model.
- The replacement hypothesis must remain explicitly provisional.
- End with the need to test whether physical preservation and functional participation differ.

## Chapter 24 — Second Check
Current function: positive and negative comparison.

Correction:
- Avoid repeating the full field-test rhythm from Chapters 20 and 23.
- Begin with Taren and Nera disagreeing about what counts as “the same system”.
- Use two adjacent sites as a visual comparison rather than narrating every measurement.
- The key discovery becomes operational: one preserved element still participates in the active route; another merely remains physically present.
- Preserve the distinction between object preservation and functional participation.
- End immediately after the test result, without a second explanatory recap.

## Chapter 25 — Insufficient Model
Current function: clean counterexample destroys the “active relation alone” model and shifts toward conditions/configuration.

Correction:
- This chapter must feel different from 19–24: quieter, more personal, more decisive.
- Start with Taren intentionally testing the strongest version of his own hypothesis.
- The negative result should arrive quickly.
- Give Nera the line that identifies the model failure; Taren accepts it without defending himself.
- The key emotional beat is relief rather than frustration: a visible wrong model is safer than an invisible wrong assumption.
- Keep the final move from “one cause” to “conditions”, but avoid another long list of facts.
- End with Taren recognizing that the investigation has changed from identifying an object/class to identifying the conditions under which the observable effect appears.

## P1 Global Rules

1. No required clue may be deleted.
2. No historical evidence may be weakened.
3. No new supernatural mechanism may be introduced.
4. No Structural Transition reveal before Chapter 32.
5. No “Trace = truth detector”.
6. No chapter may resolve the central mystery early.
7. Taren must remain fallible and method-driven.
8. Nera must remain action/route/social specialist, not Trace expert.
9. Edran must remain informed but not omniscient.
10. The reader must be able to distinguish observation from interpretation.
11. Vary scene texture: fieldwork, reconstruction, argument, controlled test, character disclosure.
12. Avoid repeated “Это доказательство?” / “Нет, пока нет” structures.
13. Keep the 15-day chronology locked.
14. Do not inflate word count merely to make chapters longer.
