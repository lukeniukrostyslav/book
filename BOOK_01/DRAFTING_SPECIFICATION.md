# BOOK 1 — DRAFTING SPECIFICATION 1.0

Status: PRE-DRAFT / READY FOR DETAILED SCENE DEVELOPMENT

## Working title
THE UNTOUCHED TRACE
Russian working title: След того, чего не было
Neither title is final.

## Narrative promise
A precise restoration specialist discovers a real relation between two things that never interacted. His investigation forces him to work with a route expert and a guarded researcher while every known model of the world begins to fail.

## Tone
Premium adult/upper-YA-compatible fantasy mystery.
Grounded, intelligent, emotionally restrained, atmospheric.
Humor comes from character behavior, not parody.
Worldbuilding is revealed through action, work, law, travel and conflict rather than encyclopedic exposition.

## POV
Primary: close third-person Taren.
Secondary POV only if structurally necessary; default is Taren to preserve mystery fairness.

## Pacing
Act I establishes anomaly and stakes.
Act II investigates, tests models and breaks false explanations.
Act III forces action under uncertainty and gives a concrete Book 1 resolution with a larger-series hook.

## Scene contract
Goal → Conflict → Outcome → New information → Consequence.
Each scene should alter at least two of information, risk, relationship, goal or interpretation.

## Mystery contract
The reader must have a genuine opportunity to reconstruct the Book 1 answer from information available before the reveal.

## Prose constraints
Avoid lore dumps, generic fantasy terminology, unexplained magical spectacle, repetitive internal monologue and fake mystery caused by withholding basic information.

Prefer physical detail, professional work, consequential dialogue, specific institutions, sensory evidence and character choices under uncertainty.

## Book 1 answer boundary
Resolved:
- impossible Trace is real;
- it is not a simple fabrication;
- known relation classes cannot adequately classify it;
- historical precedent exists;
- prior researchers encountered the same boundary;
- Taren can make a responsible decision without total certainty.

Unresolved:
- exact nature of Level III Structure;
- full reason civilizations developed incompatible models;
- whether deeper manipulation is possible or safe;
- final nature of Structural Transition.

## Draft gate
Do not begin full manuscript prose until chronology, Ch29–31 physical crisis mechanics, Trace test limitations, final Ch32 logic and the character knowledge ledger are fixed.
