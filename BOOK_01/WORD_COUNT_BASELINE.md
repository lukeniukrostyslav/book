## Important finding

The current GitHub R1 chapter files contain only about **22.8k words**, far below the planned 95–115k Book 1 manuscript range.

This is a factual repository measurement. It means we must **not** claim that Book 1 is currently a 100k-word finished novel.

The reason is also visible in the project structure: the revision layers and corrective passes contain condensed chapter versions/patches rather than a single assembled 95–115k-word canonical manuscript.

Therefore the next Final Manuscript Gate must include a **manuscript assembly + expansion pass**, not merely a mechanical merge.

## Decision lock

Do not pad the book with filler just to reach 105k.

First determine the intended full scene/chapter manuscript source, assemble all canonical material, then measure again. If the assembled story remains materially below target, expand through substantive scenes:
- character interaction
- world/civilization texture
- investigation steps
- consequences
- travel and place
- emotional beats
- fair-play clue development

Do not expand through repetitive explanation.
