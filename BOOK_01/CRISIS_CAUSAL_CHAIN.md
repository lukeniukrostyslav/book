# BOOK 1 — CRISIS CAUSAL CHAIN

Status: PRE-LOCK

## Chain
1. An old layered structure has accumulated incompatible repairs and route modifications.
2. A current transport load activates a weak physical connection.
3. The documented Trace history suggests the connection should behave differently.
4. Taren initially interprets the mismatch through the wrong model.
5. Nera identifies the actual evacuation/route constraint.
6. Edran prevents unsafe access to a protected area.
7. The team tests the smallest intervention that can stabilize the physical system without explaining the anomaly.
8. Stabilization works because it addresses the physical failure, not because the characters control Deep Structure.
9. The anomalous Trace remains after the crisis.

## Why this matters
The climax must prove the central theme:
A correct action does not require a complete theory.

## Forbidden shortcut
The crisis cannot be solved by activating a hidden power, discovering Taren is special, reversing time, or making the Trace obey a command.
