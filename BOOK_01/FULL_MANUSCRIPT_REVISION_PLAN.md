## Revision strategy
1. Preserve the existing mystery architecture and canon.
2. Expand scenes through action, sensory detail, character interaction, practical world texture, and emotional consequence — never padding.
3. Strengthen chapter-level goals and transitions.
4. Remove repeated explanatory language.
5. Maintain close-third Taren voice.
6. Keep observation separate from interpretation.
7. Preserve fair-play: no late decisive premise.
8. Audit all chronology, clue placement, knowledge states and causality.
9. Recalculate word count after each revision block.
10. Only after the complete manuscript passes final QA will Book 1 be treated as final.

## Revision passes
- Pass R1: Chapters 1–8 — opening, character establishment, mystery ignition.
- Pass R2: Chapters 9–16 — investigation escalation and midpoint.
- Pass R3: Chapters 17–24 — historical model collapse and evidence architecture.
- Pass R4: Chapters 25–32 — character transformation, crisis, ending.
- Pass R5: whole-book continuity / pacing / repetition.
- Pass R6: prose/style/originality/fair-play final gate.

Chapters 1–5.
