# BOOK 1 — TRACE TESTS AND LIMITS

## Test 1 — Restoration surface test
Purpose: determine whether the relation is embedded in the object's material history.
Limit: can identify a persistent relation but cannot prove why the relation exists.

## Test 2 — Independent specialist verification
Purpose: confirm that the first result is not a restoration artifact.
Limit: uses a different procedure and has its own uncertainty.

## Test 3 — Archive comparison
Purpose: compare Trace evidence with written provenance.
Limit: archives can be incomplete or incorrectly interpreted.

## Test 4 — Route reconstruction
Purpose: compare physical movement and known interaction pathways.
Limit: absence of a recorded route does not prove absolute non-interaction.

## Test 5 — Historical analogue
Purpose: compare current anomaly with older documented cases.
Limit: similarity does not prove identical mechanism.

## Test 6 — Counterexample testing
Purpose: test whether environmental/Trace density theories predict anomalies.
Limit: correlation is not causation.

## Book 1 rule
At least two independent methods must support any major factual conclusion.
No test can directly reveal "what reality really is."
