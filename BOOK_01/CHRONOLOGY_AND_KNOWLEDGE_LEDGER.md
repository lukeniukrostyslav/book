Meridian Cross and the Territory of Layers are close enough for the Book 1 investigation to reach the site without an implausible multi-day expedition. No exact mileage is canonized yet; prose may establish route duration without contradicting the 15-day schedule.

## Scene-level knowledge locks

### Ch1
Taren knows the object history and standard Trace method. He does not know the anomaly.
### Ch2
Taren knows the anomaly survives ordinary cleaning. He still assumes a conventional explanation.
### Ch3
Taren knows an independent test reproduces the result. He cannot yet classify it.
### Ch4
Taren learns the archive disagrees with the physical evidence.
### Ch5
Taren learns Joren has been looking into archival discrepancies. He does not know Joren's full motive.
### Ch6
Taren learns Edran recognizes the sign and wants access restricted. Edran does not reveal the old case.
### Ch7
Nera learns the anomaly conflicts with route history; Taren learns the route problem is materially urgent.
### Ch8
The pair establishes that physical route history and Trace history diverge.
### Ch9
The reconstruction explains part of the discrepancy, creating a plausible but incomplete model.
### Ch10
Joren's unauthorized research is exposed; his information is useful but not authoritative.
### Ch11
A second confirmation establishes repeatability. The local explanation is no longer sufficient.
### Ch12
The Right of Trace establishes an ethical/legal boundary: proof does not equal permission.
### Ch13
Taren sees the cost of waiting for certainty. Nera sees that action without enough information can also hurt.
### Ch14
A fragment provides an older version of the same sign.
### Ch15
Edran confirms a historical precedent existed.
### Ch16
A second impossible relation eliminates the theory that the first case is unique.
### Ch17
Taren discovers records of the Great Synthesis attempt.
### Ch18
The records show distributed knowledge and incompatible interpretations, not a simple erased truth.
### Ch19
Territory of Layers becomes the physical test site for competing models.
### Ch20
Trace density appears correlated with anomalies, but correlation is not yet mechanism.
### Ch21
Evidence temporarily supports the density hypothesis; Taren and Nera's trust increases.
### Ch22
Edran warns that the hypothesis could justify dangerous interventions.
### Ch23
A high-density location lacks the predicted anomaly.
### Ch24
A low-density location produces a relevant anomaly. The density model collapses.
### Ch25
Taren recognizes the same reasoning failure that caused his earlier personal mistake.
### Ch26
Taren withdraws instead of forcing certainty; Nera must act without him.
### Ch27
The group separates observation from interpretation as an explicit working method.
### Ch28
Edran releases the old report. It proves an unknown-side relation was observed, not what it means.
### Ch29
A physical failure creates immediate danger. Nobody gains a new supernatural ability.
### Ch30
Known/probable/unknown/risk are separated before action.
### Ch31
The smallest safe physical intervention succeeds. The anomaly survives.
### Ch32
A final independent Trace changes the question from a missing event to the possibility that no ordinary event existed between the endpoints.

## Knowledge leak prohibition
No character may establish Level III Structure, Deep Structure consciousness, or Structural Transition as fact during Book 1.
