# BOOK 1 ARCHITECTURE

Working title: THE UNTOUCHED TRACE
Russian working title: След того, чего не было
Status: WORKING

## Central mystery
How can a real relation exist between two things that never interacted?

## Character question
Can Taren make a responsible decision without complete understanding?

## Required Book 1 resolutions
- Trace is real.
- The anomaly is not a fake.
- It is not a standard known relation class.
- A historical analogue exists.
- Someone already faced a similar case.
- Edran hid part of the history.
- Taren learns to act without absolute certainty.

## Ending
The local crisis is resolved, but the mystery expands. Multiple independent traces point toward an absent event/structural transition. Book 1 does not fully explain the nature of the phenomenon.
