## Editorial problem addressed
Chapter 28 ends on a historical/model discovery. Chapter 29 begins with a physical crisis. The transition must feel causal rather than episodic.

## Locked causal bridge

1. Ch28 establishes that the old classification system is incomplete.
2. Taren, Nera and Edran therefore agree that the next useful step is not another abstract classification debate, but a field comparison in the Territory of Layers.
3. Nera's route knowledge identifies an active transport section where the old layered structure is still carrying present-day loads.
4. Edran's restricted information makes the inspection time-sensitive: the site contains the kind of layered construction represented in Ark's historical cases.
5. Ch29 begins immediately with the team at that active section, inspecting the exact physical configuration.
6. The physical failure is NOT caused by Trace or the anomaly. It is ordinary structural failure under changed load conditions.
7. The anomaly becomes relevant only because its Trace pattern does not match the documented physical history.
8. This preserves the separation between physical causality and the mystery.
