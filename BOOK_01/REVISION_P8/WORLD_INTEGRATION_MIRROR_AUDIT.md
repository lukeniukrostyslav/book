## WORLD INTEGRATION AUDIT

### 6. Geography
STATUS: STRONG / NEEDS FINAL ASSEMBLY CHECK.
Meridian Cross, South Bank, routes and Territory of Layers are functional locations, not decorative names. Exact geography remains intentionally restrained.

### 7. Civilizations
STATUS: PARTIAL.
The League/Trace worldview, Linked Cities route logic, Guardians' information-control philosophy and Incomplete School epistemic attitude are represented through characters and institutions. However, Book 1 does not yet give enough everyday material evidence that the five civilizations are materially distinct to a first-time reader.

Required integration:
- one or two concrete customs/material practices per relevant civilization;
- show institutions through work, law, trade or access decisions rather than encyclopedia exposition;
- do not add all five cultures merely for completeness.

### 8. Philosophy
STATUS: STRONG.
Philosophy is already embedded in choices:
- proof vs meaning;
- action vs waiting;
- protection vs informational control;
- admitting uncertainty.
This is preferable to direct philosophical lectures.

### 9. Economy / money
STATUS: WEAK / OPTIONAL INTEGRATION.
The world has a developed economic model, but Book 1's central investigation does not require a currency explanation.
Required integration:
- at least one natural transactional/economic detail where it also advances scene reality;
- no standalone currency exposition.

### 10. Animals / ecology
STATUS: WEAK.
The creature system exists in the World Bible but is not yet sufficiently visible in Book 1.
Required integration:
- 1–2 natural ecological details in appropriate travel/river/market scenes;
- no creature catalogue;
- no creature should become a mystery-solving device unless already canonically motivated.

### 11. Law / privacy / Trace rights
STATUS: STRONG.
The Guardians' privacy principle and the distinction between Trace evidence and guilt are already structurally relevant.

### 12. History
PASS.
Historical cases, Ark, reclassification and Great Synthesis failure directly affect the investigation.

### 13. Material culture
STATUS: PARTIAL.
Restoration, layered construction, route infrastructure and archival objects provide material grounding. Final assembly should add only details that arise naturally from characters interacting with the world.

### 14. Cosmology
PASS.
Book 1 intentionally withholds deeper cosmological explanations. This is a feature, not a gap.
