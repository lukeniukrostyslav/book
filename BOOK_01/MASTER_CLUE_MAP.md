## Fair-play rule
No critical final explanation may depend on a fact introduced only at the reveal.

## Three-layer clue discipline
Each major reveal should have observable evidence, an earlier interpretation that can be corrected, and at least one independent supporting clue.

## Red herrings
Red herrings must be true or plausible within the world and character behavior. They cannot exist only to trick the reader.
