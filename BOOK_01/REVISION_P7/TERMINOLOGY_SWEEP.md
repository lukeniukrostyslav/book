## Canonical terms

### Core mystery
- **Trace** — capitalized when referring to the in-world technical phenomenon/system.
- **relation** — lowercase when used generically.
- **relationship** — interpersonal or ordinary-language usage; do not use interchangeably with technical Trace terminology unless context requires it.
- **interaction** — a specific established relation category, not a universal synonym for relation.
- **Sequence** — established relation category.
- **Origin** — established relation category.
- **Structure** — Level III concept; not treated as proven mechanism in Book 1.
- **Deep Structure** — deeper cosmological concept; not conscious, not a god, not proven to be the source of every anomaly.
- **Structural Transition** — Book 2+ working hypothesis; never present in Book 1 as established fact.
- **unknown-side relation** — use when referring to an unresolved relation whose other side cannot be classified; avoid implying a hidden person/object.

### Investigation language
Preferred:
- **observed / observation**
- **verified / verification**
- **independent confirmation**
- **hypothesis**
- **candidate condition**
- **probable**
- **unknown**
- **risk**
- **classification**
- **category boundary**

Avoid unless technically intended:
- “proof” as a synonym for any Trace
- “cause” when only correlation exists
- “truth” when the text means interpretation
- “magic” as a casual synonym for Trace
- “connection” as a universal replacement for relation

## Technical distinction lock

### Trace is not:
- a truth detector;
- guilt detection;
- motive detection;
- omniscience;
- a universal power;
- a source of physical causality;
- a solution to every mystery.

### Trace is:
- an observable relation phenomenon;
- testable by multiple methods;
- interpreted through human classification;
- limited by access, understanding and control;
- capable of exposing a mismatch between observation and model.

## Book 1 reveal-language lock

Use:
**“We proved the relation. We did not prove that we understood its nature.”**

Do not upgrade this into:
- “We discovered the true nature.”
- “We found the hidden event.”
- “We discovered the Deep Structure.”
- “Everything is connected.”
