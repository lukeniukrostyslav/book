# RESEARCH NOTES — MYSTERY CRAFT

Current design principles used:
- Critical facts needed for a fair-play solution should appear before the reveal.
- Red herrings should be genuine details with independent explanations, not arbitrary lies inserted only to mislead.
- The ending should recontextualize earlier evidence rather than import an unexplained new fact.
- Scene-by-scene planning should track goal, conflict, outcome, clue and consequence.

External research was consulted during development. Sources include current mystery-writing guidance from NovelMint, Noren, Authr and related craft references.

This research supports craft decisions; it is not part of the fictional canon.
