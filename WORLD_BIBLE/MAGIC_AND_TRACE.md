# TRACE / MAGIC SYSTEM

## Core laws
1. Relations objectively exist.
2. Ordinary people mainly perceive consequences.
3. Magic can detect certain existing relations.
4. Magic cannot create arbitrary relations from nothing.
5. Magic cannot create matter.
6. Magic cannot simply undo completed events.
7. No absolute future prediction.
8. Knowing a relation does not equal control over its endpoints.
9. Access, understanding, and control are distinct.
10. Deeper relations become harder for human categories to interpret.

## Relation levels
I. Objects
II. Relations / Trace
III. Structure / possible structural relations
IV. Unknown relations
V. Unknown deepest foundation

## Known relation classes
- Origin
- Interaction
- Sequence
- Belonging

## Structural Transition — CANON CANDIDATE
A phenomenon where the structure of relations changes without a conventional event/object that can be identified as its source. This may explain Book 1's impossible Trace.

## Limits
Trace is not a truth detector.
Trace proves certain relations; it does not automatically prove motive, meaning, guilt, or causation.
