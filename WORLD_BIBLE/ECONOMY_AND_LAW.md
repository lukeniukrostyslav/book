# ECONOMY, MONEY AND LAW

## Money
Two-level system:
- local currencies for salaries, taxes and daily trade;
- standardized interregional trade units backed by weight, composition, issuer reputation and verifiable history.

Trace does not magically create value or money.

## Trace economy
Industries include:
- provenance certification
- Trace auditing
- archive services
- forensic analysis
- construction verification
- insurance
- confidential information protection
- route and infrastructure analysis
- Trace laundering countermeasures

## Law
Core distinction:
Trace proves that a relation exists. It does not automatically prove motive, guilt, ownership in every legal sense, or meaning.

### League law
Evidence must be reproducible and independently checkable.

### Linked Cities law
Obligations can be distributed through networks of connection.

### Dominions law
Intention, foreseeability and consequences are separately considered.

### Guardians law
Private Trace has protected status; inspection requires legal grounds or consent, subject to defined exceptions.

### Incomplete School law
False certainty about unknown relations is a serious professional/legal offense.

## Privacy
"Right of Trace" is a major civil principle. Trace access is not automatically public.

## Black markets
Trace laundering, forged provenance, illegal access and unauthorized archive modification exist.

## Inequality
Access to multiple independent verifications is a class issue.
