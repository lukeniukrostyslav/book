# WORLD OVERVIEW

The world is built around a natural phenomenon called Trace: persistent relations left by interactions and structures.

Trace is not merely a combat magic system. It became part of:
- law
- forensics
- trade
- provenance
- architecture
- economics
- privacy
- research
- education
- religion/philosophy
- political power

Human societies have developed different institutional interpretations of the same underlying phenomenon.

## Five major cultural systems
1. League of Trace — proof/provenance.
2. Linked Cities — routes/connections.
3. Dominions of Consequences — what follows actions.
4. Guardians of Untraceability — privacy/right to remain unobserved.
5. Incomplete School — disciplined uncertainty.

These are cultural/institutional systems, not five isolated fantasy races or necessarily five kingdoms. Mixed cities and populations are normal.

## Core philosophical principle
No civilization possesses the complete model. Multiple models can be locally useful while remaining globally incomplete.
