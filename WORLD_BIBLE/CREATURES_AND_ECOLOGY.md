# CREATURES AND ECOLOGY

## Principle
Biology remains real: food, water, energy, reproduction, habitat, disease, predation and mortality matter. Trace influences behavior/ecology but does not replace biology.

## Core species
### Следоход / Trace-walker
Herd animal sensitive to stable relationships and returning to old water routes. Important for seed dispersal and trail ecology. Not an oracle.

### Пепельник / Ashling
Nocturnal scavenger associated with old settlements and interaction-dense sites. Environmental indicator.

### Узловой хищник / Node Predator
Large predator that tends to hunt at intersections of stable animal routes.

### Речной стекольник / River Glass-maker
Semi-aquatic animal nesting in stable river interaction zones. Its decline can indicate river-system disruption.

### Разрывник / Breaker
Small organism/fungus-like pest that disrupts old Trace in buildings and materials. Relevant to preservation industries.

### Непарный / Unpaired
Animal that responds to unclassifiable relations. Not intelligent, not an oracle, not evidence of another dimension.

### Серебряный скат / Silver Ray
Medium marine predator of the Inland Sea, roughly 1–1.5m.

### Глубинный фильтр / Deep Filter
Large stationary filter organism/colony on the seabed, affecting water quality and fishery ecology.

## Ordinary animals
Horses, donkeys/mules, sheep, cattle, goats, dogs, cats and other ordinary fauna remain common.

## Domestication
Trace-walker is not fully domesticated.
River Glass-maker may be used as an ecological indicator rather than a magical tool.

## Ecosystem consequence rules
Removing a predator can increase herbivores and vegetation pressure.
Pollution can reduce River Glass-maker populations and affect fisheries.
Removing Breakers can cause dead organic material and old Trace accumulation in structures.
Disrupting Deep Filters can reduce water quality and fisheries.

## Hard rules
- No creature is an oracle.
- No animal explains the central mystery.
- No creature replaces human investigation.
