# DEEP CULTURES 3.0

Status: WORKING / PARTIAL LOCK

## 1. League of Trace
Maxim: "First check." / "What can we prove?"
Specialization: object-to-object provenance and reproducible Trace.
Economy: metals, materials, tools, certified goods, archives, Trace audits.
Government: councils of guilds, archives, courts, trade interests and civic representatives.
Law: reproducible evidence matters; Trace proves relation, not guilt, motive or meaning.
Politics: institutional verification and access to evidence are central.
Problem: can confuse proof with truth.
Black market: Trace laundering.
Social tension: wealthy groups can afford repeated independent verification while poorer people may have weaker access to evidence.
Ritual: Day of First Verification.

## 2. Linked Cities
Maxim: "Remember you are not first." / "What is it connected to?"
Specialization: routes, infrastructure, transport, storage and intercity connections.
Economy: shipping, transport, warehouses, brokerage, insurance and route information.
Government: union/assembly of cities; ports, bridges, warehouses and route institutions carry power.
Law: obligations and consequences are interpreted through networks of connection.
Architecture: bridges, canals, docks, markets, layered warehouses.
Problem: tendency to preserve connections that should sometimes be broken.
Ritual: Day of Return.

## 3. Dominions of Consequences
Maxim: "Think what comes next." / "What follows?"
Specialization: event-to-consequence analysis.
Economy: engineering, planning, risk analysis, legal analysis, military planning and forecasting.
Government: civic chambers and consequence assessments before major decisions.
Law: intention, foreseeability and consequence are distinguished.
Problem: can judge actions too heavily by results.
Ritual: Day of Consequences.

## 4. Guardians of Untraceability
Maxim: "Not everything knowable belongs to you." / "Do we have the right to know?"
Specialization: privacy, restricted Trace, confidential storage, diplomacy and protected information.
Economy: security, confidential archives, banking, diplomacy and private protection.
Government: contractual-republic traditions; state access to private Trace is constrained by law.
Law: unauthorized Trace inspection is illegal; exceptions exist for defined public dangers.
Factions: Restrictors, Exceptions, Absolutists.
Problem: protection can become information control.
Ritual: Day of Closed Doors.

## 5. Incomplete School
Maxim: "If you cannot name it, do not pretend to understand it."
Specialization: unknown-side relations and disciplined uncertainty.
Economy: research, education, archive analysis and specialist consulting.
Government: autonomous institutes and a Conclave with advisory authority.
Law: falsely presenting unknown as certain is a serious violation.
Education: rewards precise distinction between known, probable and unknown.
Factions: Cautious, Seekers, Radicals.
Problem: excessive caution can become paralysis.
Ritual: Day of the Unnamed.

## Shared world
These are not five species and not necessarily five separate kingdoms. Mixed populations, migration, marriages, trade and shared cities are normal. Institutions from several traditions can coexist in one city.

## Social class
Class differences arise from access to education, archives, legal services, transport, protected information and independent verification. Trace does not eliminate inequality; it creates new forms of it.

## Religion/philosophy
League: meaning through provable trace.
Linked Cities: people exist through relationships.
Dominions: responsibility includes consequences.
Guardians: people have a right to private life.
Incomplete School: honest uncertainty is intellectual integrity.

## Death
Death is final. Legacy and relationships remain; no soul-return mechanism is part of current canon candidate.

## Intercultural conflict
Conflicts usually arise because each institution treats its own useful model as more complete than it really is. No single culture is secretly the full truth.
