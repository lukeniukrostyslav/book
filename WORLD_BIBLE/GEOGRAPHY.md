# GEOGRAPHY AND WORLD MAP

## Physical structure
One main continent with adjacent territories and islands for Book 1.

Major features:
- great mountain belt across the continent;
- large river system originating in mountains;
- inland sea;
- western protected bays/coast;
- high plateaus and closed valleys;
- major trade crossroads.

## Climate
- north: cool temperate
- central river valley: fertile
- inland sea: milder
- west: maritime and wet
- plateaus: colder
- leeward zones: drier

## Civilization distribution
- Great River Valley: League of Trace influence.
- High Plateau beyond mountains: Dominions influence.
- Inland Sea: Linked Cities network.
- Western protected bays: Guardians influence.
- isolated high plateaus/valleys: Incomplete School institutes.

These are zones of cultural/institutional influence, not exclusive ethnic borders.

## Meridian Cross
Central crossroads city:
- river access
- trade routes
- mountain-pass connections
- old layered urban fabric
- mixed institutions and populations

Potential Book 1 home setting for Taren.

## Territory of Layers
Region with unusually dense historical construction and interactions:
- old roads
- rebuilt structures
- overlapping infrastructure
- multiple cultural periods
- complex Trace histories

It is important for Book 1 investigation.

## Resources
River valley: grain, vegetables, timber, crafts.
Mountains: metals, stone, minerals.
Inland sea: fish, salt, trade.
West: maritime trade, timber, fish, shipbuilding.
Plateaus: wool, livestock, herbs, stone.

## Travel
Travel is constrained by terrain, weather, roads, rivers and political borders. No instant movement.
