# COSMOLOGY

The world is objectively real.

Humanity currently models:
I. Objects — people, animals, buildings, rivers, stones.
II. Relations — Trace.
III. Structure — possible deeper organization that stabilizes objects/relations.
IV. Unknown relations — relationships human categories cannot represent.
V. Unknown — deliberately unresolved.

Deep Structure is not currently defined as a god, mind, or conscious entity.

## Absolute boundaries
- Past cannot be rewritten.
- No time travel.
- Death is final.
- No resurrection/copying of persons.
- Future is not fixed and cannot be absolutely prophesied.
- No chosen hero is required by cosmology.

## Series direction
The deeper question is not simply whether everything is connected. The question is what humans mean by an object, relation, event, and change when their categories stop fitting observed reality.
