# SERIES ARC — BOOKS 1–5

## Book 1
Question: Can a real relation exist without an interaction?
Plot: Taren's impossible Trace investigation in Meridian Cross and the Territory of Layers.
Answer: the anomaly is real, historical and outside known classes.
Open: the phenomenon may involve structural transition rather than an ordinary event.

## Book 2
Question: Why do civilizations see the world differently?
Plot: broader travel, institutions and conflicting models.
Answer: multiple models are locally useful and partially correct.
Open: why did humanity fail to unify them?

## Book 3
Question: Why did the Great Synthesis fail?
Plot: historical investigation and competing interpretations of the failed unification.
Answer: known models could not represent a deeper class without forcing it into incorrect categories.
Open: whether deeper structure can be interacted with safely.

## Book 4
Question: What happens when humans manipulate deeper structure?
Plot: moral/civilizational conflict over use, restriction and experimentation.
Answer: deeper manipulation has consequences that ordinary relation work cannot contain.
Open: what the underlying structure actually is.

## Book 5
Question: What should humanity do when its model of reality is incomplete?
Plot: large-scale conflict and a final human choice under uncertainty.
Answer: the series resolves the human/moral question without requiring a simplistic "everything is connected" metaphysical slogan.

## Series architecture rule
Each book must have its own concrete plot and resolution. Each book may answer one layer while opening another.
