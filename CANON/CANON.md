## Current CANON CANDIDATES

### World
- The world is objectively real.
- No dream/simulation/parallel-world foundation.
- Past is irreversible.
- No resurrection, time travel, absolute prophecy, or chosen-one mechanism.
- Humanity's model of reality is incomplete, not simply false.

### Trace
- Interactions can leave persistent real relations.
- Most people perceive consequences, not relations directly.
- Knowing a relation does not automatically reveal its meaning.
- Trace does not create matter or rewrite history.
- Known relation classes are incomplete.
- Wrong interpretation can be more dangerous than ignorance.

### Deep mystery
Some Trace may reflect a structural transition rather than an ordinary interaction. This is a CANON CANDIDATE, not yet final canon.
